Duskraven Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 06, 2025

At Duskraven, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We are committed to protecting your information in compliance with applicable laws, including GDPR and CCPA where relevant.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Info: Email, phone (optional), profile name, username, and profile picture.
  • Content: Posts, comments, messages, product listings, photos/videos you upload, and user-generated content (UGC).
  • Usage: App interactions, feature usage (anonymous analytics), device info (OS, IP for security).
  • Location: Only if enabled for job search or local discovery (optional and precise only when needed).
  • Payment Info: Processed securely via Apple Pay; we don't store card details.

We do not collect data from children under 13 and comply with COPPA—no targeted ads or tracking for minors.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide core app features (display content, enable chats, process bookings/subscriptions).
  • Improve the app (anonymous analytics for bug fixes and feature enhancements).
  • Moderate UGC (filter objectionable content, review reports within 24 hours, remove violations).
  • Send notifications (app updates, replies—if opted in).
  • Personalize experience (recommend jobs/listings based on activity).

Data retention: Personal data kept only as long as needed (e.g., account active); deleted upon request or inactivity (after 1 year).

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No cross-app/website tracking or advertising IDs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No sensitive data (health, biometrics) unless explicitly provided and consented.

4. Sharing & Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase (Google Cloud, compliant with ISO 27001). We only share with:

  • Service providers (hosting, moderation tools—bound by contracts).
  • Legal obligations (court orders, law enforcement).
  • No third-party advertisers or trackers.

Security measures: Encryption in transit/rest, access controls, regular audits. Report vulnerabilities to support@duskraven.com.

5. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or export your data (via app settings).
  • Opt out of notifications or data processing.
  • Delete account and data (Settings → Account → Delete—permanent, irreversible).
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent anytime.

Account and Data Deletion

Request deletion in-app (Settings → Log Out → Delete Account). We'll remove all personal info, UGC, and logs within 30 days. Anonymized aggregates may remain for analytics. For manual requests, email privacy@duskraven.com with verification.

6. Third-Party Links

App may link to external sites (e.g., payment processors). We're not responsible for their privacy—review their policies.

7. Changes to Policy

We'll notify via app/email for material changes. Continued use means acceptance.
